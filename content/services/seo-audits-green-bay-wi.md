      A useful SEO audit requires more than running a website through a tool and exporting the results. We combine automated data, manual review, local competitor research and business context to understand what is limiting visibility and what should happen next.
    steps: 
      - number: 1
        heading: Website Review
        body: >-
          We begin with the pages that matter most to customers and search engines.


          This usually includes:

          
          * Homepage
          
          * Main service pages
          
          * Location pages
          
          * Service-area pages
          
          * Contact page
          
          * Navigation
          
          * Calls to action
          
          * Conversion paths


          We examine keyword targeting, local relevance, content depth, internal links, metadata, trust signals, mobile usability, performance and common accessibility concerns. For larger websites, we also look for patterns across the site rather than treating every page as an isolated issue.
      - number: 2
        heading: Technical and Foundation Audit
        body: >-
          Next, we check for technical problems that may interfere with crawling, indexation, usability or performance. Depending on the audit scope, this may include:

          
          * Index coverage
          
          * XML sitemaps
          
          * Duplicate pages
          
          * Broken links
          
          * Site architecture
          
          * Page speed
          
          * Mobile rendering
          
          * Structured data


          We manually validate important findings because automated tools can flag issues that are technically present but unlikely to have much business impact.
      - number: 3
        heading: Google Business Profile Review
        body: >-
          For local businesses, the Google Business Profile is treated as a central part of the audit. We review:

          
          * Primary category
          
          * Secondary categories
          
          * Reviews
          
          * Competitor review counts
          
          * Photos
          
          * Services
          
          * Products
          
          * Posts
          
          * Business information
          
          * Website links
          
          * Profile activity


          We also compare the profile with local competitors to find gaps that may affect Google Maps visibility and customer trust.
      - number: 4
        heading: Competitors Analysis
        body: >-
          We look at the businesses currently showing up for the services the client wants to promote. This helps us identify:

          
          * Competitor strengths
          
          * Missing service pages
          
          * Weak location targeting
          
          * Content gaps
          
          * Review differences
          
          * Category differences
          
          * Quick-win keywords
          
          * Pages already close to stronger rankings


          For campaign clients, this review is supported by keyword research, Google Search Console data and a closer look at the website’s current authority.
      - number: 5
        heading: We Prioritize the Findings
        body: >-
          Not every issue deserves immediate attention. We organize the findings based on:

          
          * Likely business impact
          
          * Ranking opportunity
          
          * Conversion impact
          
          * Difficulty
          
          * Required time
          
          * Existing performance
          
          * Business priority


          We usually begin with low-hanging fruit, especially pages that already rank near page one or receive impressions for useful searches.


          A page ranking in positions six through fifteen may offer a faster path to measurable improvement than a new page targeting a highly competitive term from scratch.
      - number: 6
        heading: Video Walk Through of Recommendations
        body: >-
          Prospective clients receive an approximately 30-minute video audit explaining the main findings and recommended next steps.


          For ongoing clients, the audit becomes the foundation for a broader SEO strategy, content plan and implementation roadmap.


          The goal is to make the recommendations understandable enough that the business can make an informed decision, whether it works with us or completes the improvements internally.

      - question: What Is Included in an SEO Audit?
        answer: >-
          An SEO audit includes an analysis of technical SEO, on-page optimization, content quality, backlinks, keyword targeting and user experience. The audit checks factors such as crawlability, indexing, site speed, metadata, internal links, duplicate content and broken links. It identifies problems that can limit a website’s organic search visibility and rankings.

      - question: Is the SEO Audit Free?
        answer: >-
          We offer an introductory video audit for qualified prospective clients.


          This audit is designed to provide a practical overview of the biggest opportunities we see across the website and Google Business Profile. It's not a full SEO strategy, but it should give you useful recommendations you can act on whether you hire us or not.


          More detailed audits involving keyword research, Search Console analysis, competitor research, content planning and implementation strategy are typically part of a paid SEO engagement.

      - question: What Will I Receive After the Audit?
        answer: >-
          You'll receive an approximately 30-minute video walkthrough showing the main issues and opportunities we found. Rather than sending an automated report filled with technical warnings, we explain:


          * What we found
          
          * Where we found it
          
          * Why it may matter
          
          * Which opportunities appear most important
          
          * What you may want to work on first


          The video is yours to review, share with your team or give to your web developer.

      - question: How Long Does an SEO Audit Take?
        answer: >-
          The time required depends on the size and condition of the website.


          A smaller local service website usually takes less time to review than a multi-location website with hundreds of pages, several service areas and multiple Google Business Profiles.


          For deeper campaign audits, the timeline can also depend on access to Analytics, Search Console, the website CMS and other reporting platforms.


          We will explain the expected scope before beginning any paid audit or strategy work.

      - question: Do You Use Automated SEO Audit Tools?
        answer: >-
          Yes, but we do not rely on them alone.


          Automated tools can help identify technical warnings, missing elements, crawl issues and performance concerns. However, they cannot fully understand your business priorities, customer intent, local competition or which services matter most to your revenue.


          Every audit includes manual review and professional judgment. We focus on the issues that may affect visibility, usability and leads rather than treating every automated warning as equally important.

      - question: How Often Should a Website Be Audited?
        answer: >-
          A full audit doesn't need to happen every month. However, it may be worth reviewing your SEO after:

          
          * A website redesign
          
          * A platform migration
          
          * A sudden ranking decline
          
          * A major Google update
          
          * A change in services
          
          * The addition of new locations
          
          * A long period without SEO work
          
          * A change in marketing providers


          Businesses running an ongoing campaign should also review performance and priorities regularly so the strategy can adjust as rankings, competition and business needs change.
