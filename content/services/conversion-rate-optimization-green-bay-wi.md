    questions: 
      - question: What Is Conversion Rate Optimization?
        answer: >-
          Conversion rate optimization (CRO) is the process of increasing the percentage of website visitors who complete a desired action, such as making a purchase, submitting a form or signing up. CRO uses data analysis, user research and testing to identify barriers and improve website elements that influence conversions.

      - question: What Counts as a Conversion for a Local Business?
        answer: >-
          A conversion for a local business occurs when a potential customer completes a valuable action, such as calling the business, submitting a contact form, booking an appointment, requesting a quote, making a purchase or visiting the location. The specific conversion depends on the business’s goals and customer journey.

      - question: Does Conversion Rate Optimization Help SEO?
        answer: >-
          CRO helps SEO indirectly by improving how visitors interact with a website after arriving from search results. Conversion rate optimization can improve usability, content clarity, navigation and page experience. However, a higher conversion rate is not itself a Google ranking factor, so CRO does not directly increase search rankings.

      - question: How Do You Know What Visitors Are Doing on My Website?
        answer: >-
          When possible, we use website analytics along with Microsoft Clarity. Clarity gives us access to heatmaps, click behavior and session recordings that can show how people are actually interacting with your pages.

      - question: What Pages Should I Optimize First?
        answer: >-
          We typically start with the homepage and then move to the service pages most closely connected to lead generation. These pages often have the biggest opportunity to affect how prospective customers understand the business and decide whether to contact you.

      - question: Can CRO Help My Google Ads Campaigns?
        answer: >-
          CRO can help Google Ads campaigns by increasing the percentage of ad visitors who become leads or customers. Improve conversion rates by optimizing landing page relevance, calls to action, forms, page speed and usability. Higher conversion rates can generate more conversions from the same advertising traffic and reduce cost per acquisition.
