        answer: >-

      - question: Do I Need Separate Pages for Every Auto Repair Service?
        answer: >-
          Create separate pages for each major auto repair service when customers search for those services individually. Dedicated pages for brake repair, oil changes, transmission repair, tire services and engine repair can target specific local searches. Combine closely related or low-demand services when separate pages would provide little unique value. We usually start by identifying the services with the strongest combination of search demand, business value and local opportunity, then prioritize those first.

      - question: Can Local SEO Help My Shop Appear in Google Maps?
        answer: >-
          Local SEO can help your auto repair shop appear more prominently in Google Maps results. Optimize your Google Business Profile, maintain accurate business information, earn customer reviews, build relevant citations and improve your website’s local relevance. Google Maps visibility also depends on relevance, distance and prominence.
          
      - question: Do Google Reviews Affect Local SEO?
        answer: >-
          Google reviews affect local SEO by influencing a business’s prominence and visibility in local search results. A steady flow of positive, authentic reviews can strengthen local credibility and improve customer engagement.       - question: How Often Should an Auto Repair Shop Update Its Google Business Profile?
        answer: >-
          An auto repair shop should review its Google Business Profile at least monthly and update business information whenever it changes. Publish a Google Post and add new photos at least weekly to keep the profile active and useful. Update hours, services, contact details and holiday schedules immediately when they change.

      - question: Should an Auto Repair Shop Invest in SEO or Google Ads?
        answer: >-
          An auto repair shop should invest in both SEO and Google Ads when the budget allows.
